# Ping

Ping is Spark's own staff-only tool for internal communication and work tracking. It learns from TECS Obs but shares no data, terminology or design with it.

## Organisation

**Spark**:
The company using the product. It has two branches: the English academy (on-site ESL classes) and the School tours programme (visiting schools from abroad, with Spanish classes).

**Staff member**:
A person employed by Spark who can sign in. Includes owners, teachers, administrative staff, maintenance and cleaning.
_Avoid_: Employee, account, personal

**Admin**:
A Staff member who manages Staff members for all of Spark and can edit or delete any Thread or Response in the Channels they can see. Admins see only the Private channels they are members of.
_Avoid_: Coordinator, COORD, superuser

**Member**:
Any Staff member who is not an Admin.

**Deactivated**:
The state of a Staff member who can no longer sign in. Everything they wrote is kept and they stay listed in their Channels, but they no longer appear when people are being picked, and their To Do and Pings are discarded.
_Avoid_: Deleted, baja

## Communication

**Channel**:
A long-lived space for one topic: a 1:1 line with a manager, a task, a project or a team.
_Avoid_: Observation, obs, group, room

**Public channel**:
A Channel that any Staff member can find and view.

**Private channel**:
A Channel that only its members can find and view.

**Channel member**:
A Staff member who belongs to a Channel. Anyone can join a Public channel and add others to it. Only Channel admins add Staff members to a Private channel. Anyone can leave a Channel, except its last active Channel admin.

**Archived channel**:
A Channel that is closed to new activity but can still be found and read by its Channel members. Channels are archived, never deleted.
_Avoid_: Deleted channel

**Channel admin**:
A member of a Channel who may change its name, description, visibility, settings and members. A Channel always has at least one active Channel admin. Channel admins do not moderate other people's Threads or Responses.
_Avoid_: Owner, responsable

**Thread**:
A sub-topic inside a Channel, started by a post that has a title and a description.
_Avoid_: Post, seguimiento, ticket

**System thread**:
A Thread that Ping itself starts to announce a change to a Channel, such as a new Channel admin or a switch between Public and Private. Nobody can respond as its author.
_Avoid_: Bot post, automatic message

**Response**:
A message sent inside a Thread to discuss its sub-topic.
_Avoid_: Reply, comment, respuesta

**Attachment**:
An image or file added to a Thread or Response. Anyone who can read that Thread can open it.
_Avoid_: Upload, document, adjunto

**Resolved thread**:
A Thread marked finished. It is hidden from its Channel unless someone searches for it or chooses to show resolved Threads, and it can be reopened.
_Avoid_: Closed, finalizada, done

**Mention**:
Naming a Staff member in a Thread or Response with @ so that they always get a Ping, whatever their mute settings. Only Staff members who can read the Thread can be mentioned.

## Attention

**Ping**:
An item telling one Staff member about other people's new activity in one Thread, with a count of what's new since they last acknowledged it. A Staff member has at most one Ping per Thread. It stays until that Staff member acknowledges it or can no longer read the Thread. (Ping is also the product's name.)
_Avoid_: Notification, alert

**Acknowledge**:
To clear a Ping by taking one of the allowed actions: mark it read, react with an emoji, mark it To Do, or send a Response. Viewing it alone does not count.
_Avoid_: Dismiss, seen

**To Do**:
A Staff member's personal list of Threads they have committed to act on. A Thread leaves the list when that Staff member marks it done or can no longer read it. A Thread on the list still gets Pings.
_Avoid_: Task, pending

**Mute**:
A Staff member's choice to stop getting Pings for Responses, either in one Thread or across a whole Channel. New Threads and Mentions still Ping them.
