# Ping

Ping is Spark's own staff-only tool for internal communication and work tracking. It learns from TECS Obs but shares no data, terminology or design with it.

## Organisation

**Spark**:
The company using the product. It has two branches: the English academy (on-site ESL classes) and the School tours programme (visiting schools from abroad, with Spanish classes).

**Staff member**:
A person employed by Spark who can sign in. Includes owners, teachers, administrative staff, maintenance and cleaning.
_Avoid_: Employee, account, personal

**Admin**:
A Staff member who invites, Deactivates and sets the role of Staff members for all of Spark, and can edit or delete any Thread or Thread Response in the Channels they can see. Admins see only the Private channels they are members of. Spark always has at least one active Admin.
_Avoid_: Coordinator, COORD, superuser

**Member**:
Any Staff member who is not an Admin.

**Invited**:
The state of a Staff member an Admin has invited who has not yet set a password. Only Admins see them, in the list of Staff members, and they can't be added to Channels or mentioned until they join. Cancelling an invite removes them entirely, because they have written nothing.
_Avoid_: Pending, unconfirmed

**Deactivated**:
The state of a Staff member who can no longer sign in. Everything they wrote is kept and they stay listed in their Channels, but they no longer appear when people are being picked, and their Saved list and Pings are discarded.
_Avoid_: Deleted, baja

## Communication

**Channel**:
A long-lived space for one topic: a 1:1 line with a manager, a task, a project or a team.
_Avoid_: Observation, obs, group, room

**Public channel**:
A Channel that any Staff member can find and view.

**Private channel**:
A Channel that only its members can find and view.

**Channel member**:
A Staff member who belongs to a Channel. Anyone can join a Public channel and add others to it. Only Channel admins add Staff members to a Private channel. Anyone can leave a Channel, except its last active Channel admin.

**Archived channel**:
A Channel that is closed to new activity but can still be found and read by its Channel members. Channels are archived, never deleted.
_Avoid_: Deleted channel

**Channel admin**:
A member of a Channel who may change its name, description, visibility, settings and members. A Channel always has at least one active Channel admin. Channel admins do not edit or delete other people's Threads or Thread Responses, but they can remove any Attachment in their Channel.
_Avoid_: Owner, responsable

**Section**:
A named group of Channels in one Staff member's sidebar, such as Teaching or Building. Each Staff member creates their own Sections and drags Channels between them, and nobody else sees them. A Channel they haven't placed sits in the default Channels section.
_Avoid_: Folder, category, group

**Thread**:
A sub-topic inside a Channel, started by a post that has a title and a description.
_Avoid_: Post, seguimiento, ticket

**System thread**:
A Thread that Ping itself starts to announce a change to a Channel, such as a new Channel admin or a switch between Public and Private. Nobody can respond as its author.
_Avoid_: Bot post, automatic message

**Thread Response**:
A message sent inside a Thread to discuss its sub-topic.
_Avoid_: Response (on its own), reply, comment, respuesta

**Attachment**:
An image or file added to a Thread or Thread Response. Anyone who can read that Thread can open it.
_Avoid_: Upload, document, adjunto

**Resolved thread**:
A Thread marked finished. It is hidden from its Channel's Thread list unless someone chooses to show resolved Threads, still appears in search, and can be reopened.
_Avoid_: Closed, finalizada, done

**Latest activity**:
When a Thread was last active: the later of when it was started and when its newest Thread Response was posted. Edits, reactions and resolving or reopening don't count.
_Avoid_: Last updated, last modified

**Mention**:
Naming a Staff member in a Thread or Thread Response with @ so that they always get a Ping, whatever their mute settings. Only Staff members who can read the Thread can be mentioned.

## Attention

**Ping**:
An item telling one Staff member about other people's new activity in one Thread, with a count of what's new since they last acknowledged it. A Staff member has at most one Ping per Thread. It stays until that Staff member acknowledges it or can no longer read the Thread. (Ping is also the product's name.)
_Avoid_: Notification, alert

**Acknowledge**:
To clear a Ping by taking one of the allowed actions: mark it read, react with an emoji, Save it, or send a Thread Response. Viewing it alone does not count.
_Avoid_: Dismiss, seen

**Saved**:
A Staff member's personal list of Threads to come back to: to read later when they have time, to write a Thread Response, or to act on. Saving a Thread Acknowledges its Ping. A Thread leaves the list when that Staff member removes it or can no longer read it. A Saved Thread still gets Pings.
_Avoid_: To Do, bookmark, task, pending

**Mute**:
A Staff member's choice to stop getting Pings for Thread Responses, either in one Thread or across a whole Channel. New Threads and Mentions still Ping them.
